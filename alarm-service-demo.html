PrimeShift Android alarm service

Continuous recording of PrimeShift 1.0.0 (24), package app.moashift.mobile, on an Android 16 emulator. Notification and exact-alarm permissions were granted before recording.

Open or download the MP4 recording

  1. In Settings → Work alarm, the user taps “Test alarm in 10 seconds”. The app confirms the scheduled test.
  2. The user returns to the Android home screen before the scheduled alarm starts.
  3. The notification shade shows the ongoing “PrimeShift alarm test” notification and its Stop action while PrimeShift is in the background.
  4. The user taps Stop. The alarm notification disappears and the foreground service ends.

This screen recording has no audio track; it demonstrates the visible scheduling, background service and Stop flow. It does not establish speaker loudness, real-device overnight reliability, or Google Play purchase behavior. Only synthetic test data is shown.

The mediaPlayback foreground service runs only while a user-scheduled alarm plays. Playback also stops automatically after approximately 60 seconds. A boot receiver reschedules alarms and does not begin audio playback on boot.
